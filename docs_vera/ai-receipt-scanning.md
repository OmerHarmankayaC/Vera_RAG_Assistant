# AI Receipt Scanning (Yapay Zeka Fiş Tarama)

Vera Finance simplifies expense entry by eliminating the need for tedious manual input through its AI Receipt Scanning feature. Instead of typing out every single item from a grocery run or restaurant visit, you simply point your device's camera at the receipt. The application instantly processes the image and extracts all the necessary data.

The intelligent scanning system automatically identifies the products, their individual prices, and the total amount paid. Furthermore, it automatically classifies each item into its respective expense category (such as groceries, dining, or household items). This guarantees that your expense analytics remain highly accurate without any extra effort on your part.

This feature integrates seamlessly with the inventory and shopping list modules, meaning that items scanned from your receipts can directly update your household stock levels. This automated flow bridges the gap between your physical purchases and your digital financial tracking seamlessly.
