# Premium Features (Premium Özellikler)

While Vera Finance offers robust tools in its base version, the Premium subscription unlocks the application's full potential for power users. Upgrading provides a completely 100% ad-free experience, allowing you to manage your finances without any distractions or interruptions.

Premium users gain access to enhanced limits and advanced analytics. This includes the ability to scan up to 15 receipts per week using the AI Receipt Scanning tool, and unlimited tracking for all your investment assets. You also unlock comprehensive Profit and Loss (P&L) analysis and Cost Basis tracking for your portfolio.

Furthermore, Premium unlocks exclusive advanced tools such as the Personal Inflation Module to track your individual cost of living. For users who need to manage their data externally or share it with accountants, the subscription also enables exporting capabilities for both PDF and CSV formats.
