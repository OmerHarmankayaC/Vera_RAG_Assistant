# Features Overview (Özellikler Genel Bakış)

Vera Finance brings together everything you need to manage your personal finances in one app. Its core features are:

* **Expense Tracking** — categorize spending, set monthly budgets per category, and get over-limit notifications, with pie charts and analytics.
* **AI Receipt Scanning** — scan receipts with your camera to auto-extract items, prices, and categories.
* **Inventory & Shopping List** — track household stock and get automatic shopping list suggestions, synced with receipt scans.
* **Investment Portfolio** — track stocks, crypto, gold, and currencies with real-time pricing, P&L, and cost basis.
* **Savings Goals** — dedicated goal trackers with an AI-powered ETA calculator and scenario simulator.
* **Financial Health Score** — a 0–100 score with anomaly detection, end-of-month projections, and personalized recommendations.
* **Personal Inflation Module** — a premium feature calculating an inflation rate based on your own spending.
* **Achievements System** — a gamified system with missions, XP, and badge levels to keep you motivated.

All core financial data is stored locally on your device for privacy. A Premium subscription unlocks higher limits, an ad-free experience, and advanced tools like the Personal Inflation Module and data export.
