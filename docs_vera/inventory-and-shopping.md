# Inventory & Shopping List (Envanter & Alışveriş Listesi)

Managing household supplies becomes a breeze with the integrated Inventory & Shopping List features in Vera Finance. The app allows you to maintain a digital record of the items you have in stock at home, so you always know exactly what you need before you head to the store, preventing over-purchasing and reducing waste.

When an item in your inventory runs low or is completely depleted, the system intelligently prompts you to add it to your built-in shopping list. This automated workflow ensures that your shopping lists are always populated with the essentials you actually need, keeping your household running smoothly.

This module works in perfect harmony with the AI Receipt Scanning feature. After a shopping trip, scanning your receipt not only logs your expenses but also automatically replenishes your digital inventory stocks based on the items purchased, keeping your records updated effortlessly.
