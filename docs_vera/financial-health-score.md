# Financial Health Score (Finansal Sağlık Skoru)

Vera Finance simplifies the complexity of personal finance by providing a centralized Financial Health Score. This score, ranging from 0 to 100, evaluates your overall financial stability at a single glance. It considers your income, expenses, savings rate, and portfolio performance to calculate a dynamic rating that reflects your current financial well-being.

The system does more than just present a number; it actively analyzes your financial behavior to detect anomalies. If there is an unusual spike in a specific spending category or an unexpected recurring charge, the app will alert you. This proactive approach helps you catch potential financial issues before they become significant problems.

Additionally, the Financial Health Score module includes end-of-month projections and category intelligence. Based on your historical data, the app generates smart, personalized recommendations on how to optimize your budget, reduce unnecessary expenses, and ultimately improve your overall score.
