# Personal Inflation Module (Kişisel Enflasyon Modülü)

The Personal Inflation Module is a premium feature designed to give you a true understanding of how economic changes affect you directly. Instead of relying on national averages, Vera Finance calculates an inflation rate tailored exclusively to your actual spending data and the specific items you purchase most frequently.

By tracking the price changes of the goods and services logged in your expense history—especially those captured via the AI Receipt Scanning feature—the app provides a highly accurate reflection of your personal cost of living adjustments. This allows you to see exactly which categories are becoming more expensive for your household.

Armed with your personal inflation rate, you can make strategic adjustments to your budget and savings plans to protect your purchasing power over time. This targeted insight ensures that your financial planning remains realistic and resilient against economic fluctuations.
