# General Terms & Privacy Policy

Vera Finance places the utmost importance on your privacy and data security while providing you with an intuitive platform for personal finance management.

## General Information

Vera Finance was created by **Ömer Harmankaya** and is published under the brand **Static Orbit** (a brand name, not a company). The app was released in **2026**.

## Data Privacy & Storage

We employ a hybrid approach to data storage to protect your privacy while ensuring the app functions seamlessly:

* **Local Storage (On-Device):** All of your core financial data—including **transactions, budgets, assets, goals, and inventory**—is stored strictly and securely on your own device. This sensitive information never leaves your phone and cannot be accessed by our servers or any third parties. You have absolute ownership of your financial footprint.
* **Cloud Storage (Internet):** Certain operational and non-financial data must be stored on our secure servers to provide our services. This includes:
  * Your **email address** and basic account information.
  * Information regarding your **usage quota**.
  * Data processed by the **receipt scanning API (collect API)**.
* **Third-Party Data Sharing:** Please be aware that your **email address** must be shared with trusted third-party companies strictly for operational purposes (such as user authentication). We do not share your local financial data with anyone.

## Terms of Service

By using Vera Finance, you acknowledge and agree to these data handling practices. You are responsible for backing up your device to prevent the loss of your locally stored financial data. We are committed to maintaining the security of the data we collect and ensuring that your financial life remains private.
