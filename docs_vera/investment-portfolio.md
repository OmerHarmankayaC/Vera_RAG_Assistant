# Investment Portfolio (Yatırım Portföyü)

Vera Finance goes beyond basic expense tracking by offering a robust investment portfolio management module. Users can effortlessly track a wide variety of assets all from a single dashboard, including traditional stocks, cryptocurrencies, precious metals like gold, and foreign currencies.

The application pulls real-time price data to give you an accurate, up-to-the-minute valuation of your entire portfolio. This eliminates the need to constantly switch between different financial apps or websites to see how your investments are performing, centralizing your wealth overview in one place.

Furthermore, the portfolio tracker provides deep Profit and Loss (P&L) analysis and calculates your cost basis automatically. By understanding your exact entry points and seeing your net gains or losses visualized, you can make smarter, data-driven decisions regarding when to hold, buy, or sell your assets.
