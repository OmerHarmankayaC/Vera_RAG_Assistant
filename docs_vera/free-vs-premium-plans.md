# Free vs Premium Plans (Ücretsiz ve Premium Planlar)

Vera Finance is available in two tiers: a fully functional Free plan and a Premium subscription that unlocks higher limits and advanced tools. This document outlines exactly what the Free plan includes and how it compares to Premium, so you can decide which fits your needs.

## Free Plan Limits (Ücretsiz Sürüm Limitleri)

The Free plan gives you full access to core budgeting, expense tracking, savings goals, inventory, and achievements — with the following limits:

* **AI Receipt Scanning:** Up to **3 receipt scans per week**.
* **Investment Portfolio:** Track a maximum of **10 assets**, with **current value only** (no Profit & Loss or cost basis analysis).
* **Personal Inflation Module:** **Not available** on the Free plan.
* **Experience:** **Ad-supported** — the app displays ads during use.

## Premium Plan (Premium Sürüm)

Upgrading to Premium raises these limits and unlocks exclusive tools:

* **AI Receipt Scanning:** Up to **15 receipt scans per week**.
* **Investment Portfolio:** **Unlimited** asset tracking, plus full **Profit & Loss (P&L)** analysis and automatic **cost basis** tracking.
* **Personal Inflation Module:** **Fully unlocked**, calculating an inflation rate tailored to your own spending.
* **Experience:** **100% ad-free**, with no distractions or interruptions.
* **Data Export:** Export your data to both **PDF and CSV** formats for external use or sharing with an accountant.

In short, the Free plan is ideal for everyday budgeting and light portfolio tracking, while Premium is built for power users who need higher scanning limits, deeper investment analytics, personal inflation insights, and an ad-free experience.
