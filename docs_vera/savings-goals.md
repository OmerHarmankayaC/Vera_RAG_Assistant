# Savings Goals (Tasarruf Hedefleri)

Whether you are saving up for a vacation, a new house, a car, or simply building an emergency fund, Vera Finance helps you structure and manage your savings effectively. You can create dedicated goal trackers for each specific objective, making it easy to monitor your progress over time as you contribute funds toward them.

A standout feature is the AI-supported Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) calculator. By analyzing your current financial habits and savings rate, the application intelligently predicts exactly when you will reach your target amount. This gives you realistic timelines and helps you adjust your behavior if you want to reach your goal sooner.

Additionally, the app features a scenario simulator that allows you to compare different contribution amounts. You can test various hypothetical saving scenarios—such as adding a bit more each month—and immediately see how it impacts your target completion date, empowering you to make informed decisions about your savings strategy.
