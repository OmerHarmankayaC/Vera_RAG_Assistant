# Platform & Availability (Platform ve Erişilebilirlik)

Vera Finance is an **iOS application** and iOS is the only platform it runs on. It is downloaded and updated through the Apple App Store. The app was created by **Ömer Harmankaya** and published under the **Static Orbit** brand in **2026**.

## Where you can use Vera Finance

Vera Finance runs on the iPhone. To use it you need an iPhone and an App Store account; there is no other way to access your Vera Finance data.

* Vera Finance is **not available on Android**. There is no Android app and no Google Play listing.
* Vera Finance is **not available on computers**. There is no website version, no browser app, no Windows application and no macOS application, so it cannot be used from a laptop or desktop computer.
* Vera Finance is **not available on any other device type**, including tablets, smart watches and televisions.

Vera Finance is an iPhone-only app. Any platform other than the iPhone is unsupported.

## Internet connectivity

Vera Finance keeps your financial life on your own device, but it is not a fully offline app. Your core financial data — transactions, budgets, assets, goals, and inventory — lives locally on your iPhone and can be viewed without a connection. An internet connection is required for the features that depend on outside services: signing in to your account, AI Receipt Scanning, and real-time pricing for your investment portfolio.

Because your financial records are stored on the device rather than in the cloud, backing up your iPhone is the way to protect them if you lose or replace your phone.
