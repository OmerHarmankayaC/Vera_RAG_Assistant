# Expense Tracking (Harcama Takibi)

Vera Finance offers a comprehensive daily expense tracking module that allows you to categorize and monitor your financial outflows effortlessly. Users can define custom budgets for different spending categories on a monthly basis, ensuring they stay within their financial limits. If a category's budget is nearing its limit or has been exceeded, the app automatically sends over-limit notifications to keep the user informed.

To provide a clear visual representation of spending habits, the expense tracking feature includes detailed pie charts and comprehensive analytics. These visual tools help you instantly see where your money is going, making it easier to identify areas where you can cut back and save. By maintaining an accurate log of daily transactions, you gain complete control over your cash flow.

All transaction data is processed locally on the device using AsyncStorage, ensuring that your financial history remains completely private and secure without being sent to external servers.
