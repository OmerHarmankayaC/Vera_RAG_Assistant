# Achievements System (Başarımlar)

To keep users motivated and engaged with their financial health, Vera Finance incorporates a gamified Achievements System. By transforming mundane financial habits into a rewarding game, the app encourages consistent budgeting, saving, and investing behaviors over the long term.

The system features 50 different distinct missions and tasks for users to complete. As you hit specific milestones—such as staying under budget for consecutive months or scanning a certain number of receipts—you earn Experience Points (XP) that contribute to your overall progression within the app.

As you accumulate XP, you can unlock up to 4 different badge levels, showcasing your mastery over your personal finances. This rewarding loop ensures that managing your money feels less like a chore and more like an engaging journey towards financial freedom.
